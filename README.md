Food wastage and food insecurity often exist side by side because there is no efficient channel connecting people who have surplus food with organizations that can redistribute it. Donors are frequently unaware of nearby NGOs in need, and NGOs lack real-time visibility into available donations, forcing both parties to depend on informal, time-consuming methods such as phone calls and social media, which are especially inefficient for perishable food.

To solve this, the proposed system, FoodRescue, is a role-based web and mobile application designed to streamline the food donation process through three primary user roles: Donor, NGO, and Admin. Donors can list surplus food items, which are instantly made visible to nearby NGOs through a real-time notification system. NGOs can browse available listings and raise requests, which then move through a defined approval workflow to ensure accountability and prevent duplicate or unauthorized claims. The Admin role provides oversight of the entire ecosystem, managing users and generating reports to track donations, requests, and overall platform impact.

The system is developed using React.js for the frontend, PHP8 for backend logic, MySQL as the database layer, and JWT/Firebase Authentication for secure, role-based access. The application is deployed using Hostinger practices for scalability and reliability.

By digitizing donation discovery, request handling, and approval into a single connected platform, FoodRescue minimizes coordination delays, increases transparency through auditable donation records, and contributes meaningfully toward reducing food waste — directly supporting SDG 2: Zero Hunger and SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production.
